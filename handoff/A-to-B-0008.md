## What

Lane A23 ran a look lab on the 4090 in a worktree (`docs/experiments/LOOK-LAB.md`, stills `docs/experiments/img/look-lab/`: `proof-*` against `base2-b-*` / `play-b-*`, same frozen
frame of hockey.regular seed 1 at 10 / 24 / 40 m; the proposal materials are on main under `Game/Content/LookLab/` with `MI_LL_*_Rec` holding the recommended values). In order of
effect, all inside +0.5 ms of your look on the 4090:

1. **Sky dome instead of the room's walls and ceiling** in `LR_L2_Night`: deep-blue gradient (`M_LL_Sky`: horizon (0.05, 0.09, 0.22), zenith (0.004, 0.006, 0.018), brightness 0.5).
   The black void goes and the dark ice lightens. This replaces the room your B2 dressing assumed; R-0021 point 4 (props out) makes that cheaper.
2. **Spot inner cone 5 deg** (outer 26.5): soft pools with wider painted rings. 25 deg gives hard discs.
3. **Ice: two posterised tiles** in `M_Ice` in front of `ScuffMask` (scuff streaks stretched 10:1 along the rink, frost patches; `M_LL_Ice2` shows the graph) and **Wet 0.3** on
   `MI_Ice_Night` for the Lumen reflections, keeping your `Ambient` floor.
4. **Outline width by distance** in `M_PP_Outline`: 3 px at 10 m to 1.5 px at 40 m, constant ink colour, inner lines from normals (`M_LL_Outline`).
5. **Figure fill 2.5 -> 1.5** (`MI_Toon_Blue/Orange` Emissive 1.5): the toon ramp's bands, softness and tint are invisible on a top-lit figure at fill 2.5 (twelve variants identical at
   24 m); the ramp reads on the pools, not the figures. The figures themselves are the weakest link against the reference and no post setting fixes it: chunkier painted figures
   with the lower fill (A-to-B-0007 point 3 and lane A22's Blender figures, coming as a separate handoff).
6. **Construction**: two instances `MI_Toon_KickPlate` (25 cm ochre) and `MI_Toon_GlassRail` (dark rail with stanchions); A's `RinkActor` draws the bands and the 14 cm red net
   frame (lane A25).
7. **Two end haze cards** at strength 0.8, nothing at the sides; no shaft cones (they wash the frame whenever the chase camera is inside a pool).

Rejected with evidence (do not spend time on them): planar reflections (4.7 ms), forced SSR (+1.7 ms, no gain), rim term, more ramp bands, side haze, shaft cones. Hardware ray
tracing (Lumen HWRT, RT reflections): +0.5 to 0.7 ms, no visible change at any distance; the painted post survives it; not worth a tier.
