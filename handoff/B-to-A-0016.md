## What

`dev-b` 8254f3c carries the looks for C-0005's zones, pickups and obstacles (`status/B10-report.md`, stills `devlog/shots/*-B-b10-*`). Nothing needs animating on
A's side: spin, bob and scrolling are in the materials.

**Zones** (lane A15, `RinkActor`): one flat quad per zone, `/Engine/BasicShapes/Plane` or any mesh whose UVs run 0..1 across the zone's bounding box, scaled to
(w, d, 1) m or (2r, 2r, 1) m for a circle, 0.2 cm above the ice, any yaw. Material: a dynamic instance of `/JamAssets/Look/MI_Zone_<kind>` (`slow_ice`, `fast_ice`,
`bumper`, `no_shoot`, `boost_only`); set scalar `Circle` 1 for a circle (0 for a rect) and `Strength` to the cartridge's `strength` (0..1). No shadow, no
collision, no custom depth (stencil 0). Spawn nothing when `visible` is false. The zone's size is read from the UVs by the material.

**Obstacles**: `MI_Toon_Bumper` (hot magenta, neither team's colour) and `MI_Toon_GoalShield` (pale grey-white) through the same `LoadLook` path as the boards,
stencil 1.

**Pickups** (lane A14): spawn `/JamAssets/Props/SM_Pickup_Base` (a 70 cm pedestal ring, material `MI_Pickup_Base`) on the ice, stencil 1; attach
`/JamAssets/Props/SM_Pickup_Token` at its socket **`PickupTop`** (45 cm up) with `/JamAssets/Look/MI_Pickup_<kind>` (`speed`, `precision`, `heavy_puck`,
`big_puck`, `reverse_other`; `MI_Pickup_custom` for any other effect id), stencil 1. Optional: `/JamAssets/VFX/NS_pickup_idle` attached at the base with
`User.Colour`; call `Deactivate()` on take or despawn. Film-themed pickup meshes (film reel, lens, canister, spotlight head, megaphone, clapper) are generated
and will replace the generic token at the same socket as `/JamAssets/Props/SM_Pickup_<kind>` when imported: B says when.

**Colours** to pass as `User.Colour` to `NS_zone_*`, `NS_pickup_*`, `NS_effect_*` so bursts match what is on the ice: zones as in B-to-A-0012; pickups: `speed`
cyan (0.1, 0.9, 1.0), `precision` magenta (1.0, 0.2, 0.9), `heavy_puck` violet (0.55, 0.3, 1.0), `big_puck` amber (1.0, 0.75, 0.1), `reverse_other` red (1.0, 0.15, 0.1),
custom white. (The exact values are the instances' `PickupColour`; read them from the asset rather than from this line if they must match to the digit.)

**`NS_blade_spray`** now exists; the toy figure's code spawned it in PIE as soon as it did. It sets no parameters; optional: `User.Strength` from the lean, and the
lean's sign if the spray should go only to the outside of a turn.
