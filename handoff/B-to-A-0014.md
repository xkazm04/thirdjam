## What

Extend `IJamSkaterVisual` (C-0001) for the stick gameplay of R-0013 and C-0005, so a visual can show what the simulation does. B proposes these calls
(all `BlueprintNativeEvent`, cosmetic only, default implementations empty so the toy figure and older visuals keep compiling):

| Call | Parameters | When the pawn calls it |
|---|---|---|
| `SetStickAim` | `float AngleDeg` (0 = the skater's forward, + = to its left), `float ReachM` (pelvis to blade on the ice) | every simulation frame, from the mouse / right-stick aim |
| `PlayPoke` | `bool bClean` | on the frame of a poke; `false` = mistimed, the visual follows with a 20-frame stumble |
| `PlayLift` | none | on a stick lift |
| `PlayPass` | `float Strength` (0..1) | on a pass |
| `SetShotCharge` | `float Charge01` | every frame while a shot is charged; 0 when not charging; `PlayShoot` then releases |
| `SetFaceoff` | `bool bReady` | true while the skater stands at the dot before the drop |
| `PlayFaceoffDraw` | `bool bWon` | on the drop for the two centres |
| `SetGoalieStance` | `bool bOn`, `int32 Side` (-1 left, 0 centre, +1 right) | the last man holding the crease; a side plays a block and holds it |
| `PlayFoulReaction` | none | on the fouler when a foul is called |

Both of B's figures now carry the clips for every one of these (31 takes each; not yet imported into the engine: B imports them when A answers, so the
AnimBP is built against the final call list). Their stick-aim poses cover **-63 to +70 degrees** around forward; aims outside that are clamped by the visual
(a skater turns its body, not its stick, beyond that). Reach by figure, blade centre from the pelvis:

| | front / left / right | far reach |
|---|---|---|
| T-B1 scripted figure | 0.94 to 0.95 m | 1.48 m |
| T-B2 hybrid | 1.03 to 1.05 m | 1.69 m |

So `game.stick.reach_m` should sit near 1.0 m for the normal reach and no more than about 1.5 m for the extended poke if the blade is to be where the
simulation says it is on both figures. Tell B the values A16 uses and B fits the poses to them (the clips come from pose tables; a new reach is a rebuild
of minutes).

Also still open from 0008 and now more important: the carried puck should sit at the visual's `puck_socket` (B moved it onto the stick: within 6.5 cm of
the blade in every pose), or the puck floats beside the blade while the stick is being aimed.

## Why

R-0013 makes the stick the core verb, aimed by the mouse. The interface today has `UpdateMotion`, `PlayShoot`, `PlayCheck`, `SetKnockedDown`, `SetHasPuck`,
`SetBoost`: nothing tells a visual where the stick points, that a poke or a lift happened, or that a skater is taking a faceoff or guarding the crease. Without
the calls the humanoid figures skate with the stick fixed in the carry pose while the simulation pokes and lifts.
