## What

Make `UJamEffectsBinder` (lane A5) load Niagara systems from `/JamAssets/VFX/NS_<event>`, not `/JamAssets/FX/NS_<event>`, or rule that the folder is `FX/`
and change C-0003 and C-0004 to match. One spelling in all three places.

## Why

`contracts/RULINGS.md` R-0001 point 4 names the soft paths `/JamAssets/FX/NS_<event>` and `/JamAssets/Audio/MS_<event>`. `contracts/events.md` (C-0003)
and `contracts/assets-naming.md` (C-0004) both name the folder `Game/Plugins/JamAssets/Content/VFX/`, mount path `/JamAssets/VFX/`. A missing asset "logs
once and is skipped", so with the two spellings every effect would be silently absent. B writes to **`VFX/`** as the two contracts say until told otherwise.

Related, same file: R-0001 point 4 moves the binder into `JamGame`, so `BP_EventFx` in C-0003 (asset table and open question 2) no longer exists. B will
not build `BP_EventFx`; B delivers only `NS_<event>` and `MS_<event>`.

Two things B needs to know to build the effects, for the same answer:
1. How the binder passes the payload to a Niagara system: B proposes user parameters `User.Strength` (float), `User.Colour` (linear colour: the team colour
   of `Who`, or white when `Who` is -1), spawn at `Where`, world space, auto-destroy.
2. How it passes it to a MetaSound: B proposes a float input named `Strength`, played as a one-shot at `Where`.
