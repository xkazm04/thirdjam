# B-to-A-0011: the look switch scales the rig with the rink, so one rig fits the 90, 120 and 140 m cuts

## What

In `ULookTierSwitcher::SetTier`, stream the rig map in with a **uniform scale `s = game.rink.length_m / 90`** (level transform of the `ULevelStreamingDynamic`
load), and after it is loaded, for every `ULocalLightComponent` in the streamed level multiply `Intensity` by `s * s` and `AttenuationRadius` by `s`.
Re-apply on a cartridge load or a Director's Call that changes the rink size.

B authors every rig for the **default 90 x 45 m rink** (R-0010) from now on: the four pools of `LR_L2_Night` are being re-laid at x = +-11.25 and +-33.75 m
today (lane B1c), and the set dressing moves in to stand 3 to 12 m outside the 90 x 45 boards next.

## Why

A rig is a fixed map (C-0002), but the rink is a cartridge value, and R-0010 moved the default from 120 x 60 to 90 x 45 after the rig was built. Today the
rig is laid out for 120 m with the dressing outside the 140 x 70 footprint: on the default rink the two outer light pools sit on the end boards, and the film
set behind each net stands 27 m beyond the boards, small and dark. If B lays the rig out for 90 x 45 instead, the dressing would stand **on the ice** of the
120 and 140 m cuts. All three shipped rink sizes are 2:1 (90 x 45, 120 x 60, 140 x 70), so one uniform scale keeps lights over the ice and the set outside
the boards at every size. Light positions scale with the level transform; a spot's pool grows with its height (same cone angle), which is what we want; its
brightness on the ice falls with the square of the height, which the `s * s` on the intensity restores (the exposure is fixed per rig). Props grow 1.33x
and 1.56x on the two big cuts; on a film set that reads as bigger gear, and B accepts it.

If A would rather not touch lights, the fallback is the scale alone: B then accepts dimmer pools on the big cuts (57 % and 41 % of the default) and says so in
the cut descriptions. If A rejects the scale as well, B needs three rig maps per tier and three cook-list lines; B asks not to go there.
