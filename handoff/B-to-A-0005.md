## Why

The painted-cel look is two post-process passes that read `CustomStencil` (`tools/skills/look-painted-cel.md` section 1): 1 = toon ramp and ink outline, 2 = banded
ramp on the ice without outline, 3 = outline only, 0 = untouched. A mesh without custom depth is stencil 0: no ramp, no outline, so the rink and the figures would
render as plain lit geometry inside B's rig. The boards, nets, ice, figures and puck are spawned by A's code, so only A can set it. The trim strips and the
beacon follow the cartridge's rink size, which is why B cannot place them in the fixed rig map.

Two parameters B needs A to drive on `MI_Ice_Night` (dynamic instance) when the rink is built, because the ice lines are painted from world position:
`RinkHalfLength` and `RinkHalfWidth` in Unreal units (cm), from `game.rink.length_m` / `width_m`. B proposes these two names; the blue lines, centre circle and
goal lines are derived from them inside the material.
