## Why

- B ran four no-editor experiments this morning to find the best humanoid (`briefs/B-skater-lab.md`). Generated meshes look good (the best is a Hyper3D Rodin
  mesh rigged in Meshy, now going through the T-B2 import path), but generated **motion** is the weak part: text-to-motion strides do not loop without
  repair, there is no stick, the wrist shot and check are unusable (`status/B-skater-s2.md`). The scripted figure has the best motion of any candidate.
- The Fab route costs a purchase by the operator, a download, a UE4-to-UE5 retarget, and yields `sourced`, unscored content. The scripted route costs
  nothing and is ours.
- Without a consumer of `game.skater.visual` neither B track can be judged against the promotion rule (player bot still wins, frame gate holds).
