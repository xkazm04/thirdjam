Asks, all optional, each makes one picture better:

1. **`User.Dir` on `check` and on `knockdown`**: a unit vector on the rink plane (world x, y; z = 0), the sim's contact normal (checker towards victim) and
   `KnockDir`. `NS_check` is radial while it is zero and throws its chips along the hit when it is set; with it on `knockdown` B adds the slide streak.
2. **`User.Normal` on `board`**: a unit vector pointing into the rink at the contact (the sim's board normal): the chips then leave the board face and the
   shake line lies along the board. Without it the burst is radial at the contact point.
3. **Blade spray cadence**: your figure respawns `NS_blade_spray` every 8 frames; with the larger fan that is about 120 chips per hard-turning skater. Every 12
   frames is enough if several skaters near the camera look busy; B's own figures follow whatever the toy does.

For information: in a 92 s bot match on B (your `main` of 18:13, before A33) the events fired as lift 26, board 19, poke 16, pass 7, faceoff 6, shot 3,
goal 2, and **check, knockdown and foul 0**: the three effects that matter most were proven only by spawning them as the binder does. Peak particles at
Strength 1.0: check 81, board (skater) 57, knockdown 54; every changed system is unlit translucent with lifetimes of 0.8 s or less; no frame cost measured on B.
