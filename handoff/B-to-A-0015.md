## Why

Everything in those four folders is loaded **by a path built at run time**, which the cooker cannot see:
`EffectsBinder.cpp` lines 28 and 33 (`FString::Printf("/JamAssets/VFX/NS_%s...")`, `"/JamAssets/Audio/MS_%s..."`), `HokejGameMode.cpp` lines 134-135
(`BP_SkaterVisual_TB1_C`, `_TB2_C`), `ToySkaterVisual.cpp` line 36 (`NS_blade_spray`), and the puck kit (`/JamAssets/Props/SM_PuckDisc`). The cook list on `main`
(`DefaultGame.ini` lines 32 to 41) has `/JamAssets/Look` and the three rig maps only.

B ran a cook-only check on device B at `dev-b` 71c3aa5 (`UnrealEditor-Cmd -run=cook -targetplatform=Windows`): 540 packages, **0 errors**, 73 s; the log mentions
`/JamAssets/Look` and `/JamAssets/Arena` (pulled in by the rig map) and has **no mention at all** of `/JamAssets/VFX`, `/JamAssets/Audio`, `/JamAssets/Skaters` or
`/JamAssets/Props`. So in every package so far the 21 Niagara systems, the 24 MetaSounds, both skater visuals and the puck disc are absent, and the binder's
"log once and skip" hides it: PIE shows everything, the shipped game is silent and effect-less. (`Arena/` is safe as long as its meshes are placed in a rig map.)

Two related things:
- `ToySkaterVisual.cpp` expects `/JamAssets/VFX/NS_blade_spray`. B never received a request for it; B builds it now (ice spray from the blades; tell B the user
  parameters the toy figure sets, or B uses `User.Strength` 0..1 and `User.Colour`).
- A package does not complete on device B (`link.exe` for the game executable hangs under x64 emulation on this ARM64 machine, twice), so B cannot verify a
  packaged build itself: B's check is the cook, A's is the package.
