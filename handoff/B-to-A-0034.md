## What

The operator made `p3_clay` the next priority tier (22:50, handoff 0032) and pointed at the art concepts `prep-reference/images/path-p3-*`. Lane B19
(`status/B19-report.md`; stills `devlog/shots/20261112-2355*-B-b19-final-*`, `-235901-B-b19-zones-low`) rebuilt the tier against the third of them: `LR_P3_Clay`
is now a plank barn with eight strings of warm bulbs, four shadowed warm key spots and six shadowless fills; the ice on this tier is **glossy and finely
scratched** (a path in `M_Ice` that is active only when `MPC_Look.Clay` is 1: night and broadcast measured unchanged), the boards are plaster panels with joints,
a yellow kick strip and a red band, zones and pickups glow warmer, the focus is 16 m at f/4 with no depth blur (the rink is sharp from the player to the far
net). The cream tabletop is gone. What limits the picture now is on A's side:

1. **Hide the glass, the glass rail and the stanchions on `p3_clay`** (the concept has open boards; A's code uses one instance per part on every tier, and
   an opaque part cannot vanish through its material: the rail and posts show as a dark fence at 2.1 m). B made the glass itself nearly invisible on this tier
   through `MI_Toon_Glass`; the rail and posts need `SetVisibility(false)` by tier (in `SetGlassForTier` or beside it). Optional: a plain red top rail instead
   of the team trim on this tier.
2. **Lumen reflections on this tier, on machines that can pay for them.** The concept's foreground ice is full of warm reflections of bulbs that hang above
   and behind the camera; screen-space reflections (what the rig's volume sets now, quality 80) cannot see them. Ask: measure `p3_clay` on the 4090 with the
   rig's post-process volume switched to Lumen reflections (B's estimate from A23's numbers: +0.5 to 0.9 ms) and, if it fits, tell B to save it that way, with
   screen-space as the fallback at the lower quality settings.
3. **Zones as opaque emissive rings on this tier.** The concept's zones and pickups are glowing clay rings and capsules that reflect in the ice; A's zones
   are translucent decals, which never appear in screen-space reflections. Ask (after 1 and 2): spawn a thin ring mesh per zone on `p3_clay` (a torus or a
   flat ring 6 cm high on the zone's outline) with B's material (`MI_Zone_<kind>` works on a mesh; B makes a ring mesh `SM_ZoneRing` if A wants one asset).
4. **Markings under the ice.** A's construction markings (faceoff circles and dots, the crease) are opaque paint on top of the ice; on the glossy clay ice
   they sit above the reflections while B's lines (centre, blue, goal lines) are under the surface. Ask: either expose the circle, dot and crease numbers as
   ice-material parameters like the lines (B paints them in `M_Ice` on every tier), or give those meshes a material B can make glossy on this tier.

For the Director: `contracts/look-tiers.md` describes `p3_clay` with a directional key; the keys are four spot lights now (they scale with the rink like the
night rig's). Also unchanged from before: the 120 x 60 m temporary floor casts shadow from under the ice (four shadowed spots see it now).
