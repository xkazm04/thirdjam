## What

Every `NS_<event>` and `MS_<event>` of C-0003 (including `directors_call`) now exists on `dev-b` under `/JamAssets/VFX/` and `/JamAssets/Audio/`, plus the
puck readability kit. Lanes A5 (binder) and A1 (puck, rink) wire them as below. Full detail and the calls that built them: `status/B3-report.md`.

1. **Event effects** `/JamAssets/VFX/NS_<event>` for `shot`, `board`, `goal`, `directors_call`, `win`, `check`, `knockdown`, `pickup_spawn`, `pickup_take`
   (there is none for `faceoff` and `lose`, by design): set `User.Strength` (float 0..1) and `User.Colour` (linear colour in 0..1, no HDR: the glow comes
   from the material), spawn at `Where`, world space. Each turns itself off within 4.5 s; spawn with auto-destroy.
2. **Event sounds** `/JamAssets/Audio/MS_<event>` for all eleven events: float input `Strength`, one-shot at `Where`. Sound class `SC_SFX` is assigned in the assets.
3. **Puck kit**, attached to the puck actor by lane A1:
   - `/JamAssets/Props/SM_PuckDisc` (60 cm x 4 mm, material `MI_Trim_PuckHalo` already assigned) as a child mesh lying on the ice under the puck, with
     `bAffectDynamicIndirectLighting = false`, `CastShadow = false`, no custom depth (stencil 0), no collision. **The first flag matters:** without it the moving
     glowing disc leaves fading ghost copies of itself on the ice under Lumen.
   - `/JamAssets/VFX/NS_puck_trail` as an attached Niagara component: `User.Speed` in **m/s** every frame (invisible under 4 m/s, full from 8 m/s) and
     `User.Colour` once (B's default accent is (0.75, 1.0, 0.15)). It never finishes by itself; deactivate it during the goal pause and faceoff reset.
4. **Loops** (not events; the binder or the game mode starts them once): `/JamAssets/Audio/MS_arena_room` at match start, no inputs, class `SC_Ambience`;
   `/JamAssets/Audio/MS_skate_loop` on the **player's** skater only, float input `Speed` as 0..1 (speed / `game.skater.speed`) every frame and trigger input `Turn`
   when `|turn_rate|` exceeds about 3 rad/s. Volume sliders in the menu: sound classes `SC_SFX`, `SC_Ambience`, `SC_Music`, mix `SCM_Volume`, all in `/JamAssets/Audio/`.

## Why

A missing or misnamed parameter is silent (R-0002 point 4: a missing asset logs once and is skipped), so the names are stated here once. Three things B
could not do from its side and asks A to decide:
- **Trim pulse on `goal` and flash on `board`:** B delivered the particles only. The trim strips are spawned by A1; a pulse means animating `Emissive` on their
  dynamic instances for about 1.2 s after `goal` (to 3x and back). B's proposal: A does it in the binder; if A would rather not, say so and B adds a
  time-driven pulse parameter to `M_Trim`.
- **`knockdown` has no direction in the payload**, so `NS_knockdown` sprays radially. That is acceptable to B; no change asked.
- `NS_directors_call` and `NS_win` ignore `User.Colour` on purpose (fixed warm white; fixed trim blue and orange).
