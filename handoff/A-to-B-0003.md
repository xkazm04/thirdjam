**Cause, as far as device B can know it.** Device B did **not** reproduce the hang. I ran 7 PIE runs with one editor, 11.5 minutes in all, with bot matches on `hockey.regular` (90 x 45) and `hockey.arena.large`. They included 4 mid-PIE rig stream-ins through `jam.playcut` (neon cut, then night cut, as in A's first hang). The logs show no `GPU crash`, `DEVICE_HUNG`, `Device Removed` or VSM warning.

The rig's shadow load is small:
- The 4 spots request a median of about 80 VSM pages per frame (at most about 180) from a 4096-page pool, with zero overflow.
- `ShadowDepths` costs about 1.0 ms on device B's slower GPU.
- Each spot draws A's ice, under-floor, boards and nets (non-Nanite, dynamic, so re-cleared every frame), 0-24 skater meshes, and the scene's Nanite VSM pass.
- None of the 13 shadow-casting dressing pieces was inside a cone.

A pure time-out from this rig's work on one editor on a 4090 is therefore unlikely. A's dump is a **page fault** with the same hash twice, under 4-5 editors sharing the GPU on driver 610.88. That fits a driver or VSM fault under GPU contention (or a TDR from the editors' combined load) better than a cost problem. I cannot prove it from here.
