SKF25 the get-ups on open ice, game camera, every 2nd frame, p2_cel (top 16 rows) then p3_clay. Per direction (from the back, face down, left side, right side) four rows on one frame scale that ends on the release (red bar at the right edge): the 36-frame get-up, the new GetUp_LateLong* (36 frames), the 20-frame GetUp_Snap*, the new GetUp_Late* (20 frames). The yellow line is where the hips pass half their standing height for good; each row's strip gives that frame and the frames from there to the release. The new takes are down or low to the yellow line (sitting, a low push-up, sitting on one hip) and up in the last 5 frames in every row; the old ones are up 25 to 31 frames (36-frame takes) and 6 to 13 frames (snap) before the release. The first 12 frames of the LateLong rows are lying with a breath and a head turn (1 to 3 cm of head travel: too small to read at this cell size). Rendered from export25 before the Stride_Sprint key was added; the get-ups are key-identical in the final files (compare_anim.py).
