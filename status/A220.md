**Why the old cards deformed the pictures.** `RefreshModes` put each picture in a `UImage` via `SetBrushFromTexture(Tex, false)`, so the brush had no size of its own. The image sat in `Sized(0, 88)`, a slot set to Fill/Fill, inside a uniform-grid cell 320 units wide (1680 / 5, less 2 x 8). As a result every picture was painted at 320 x 88 (3.636:1). Device B's pictures are all 640 x 360 (16:9, 1.778), so each was shown at 2.05 x its width-to-height, squashed to 49 % of its own height. Nothing in that chain kept the texture's proportions.
