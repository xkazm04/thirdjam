## Decisions (each with its number)
1. **Boards are a body** (`UJamSim::BoardFactor`, read in `ResolveContact`): victim's centre within `ContactBoardRangeM` 1.5 m of the boards AND the push runs into them:
   impulse x `1 + (board_bonus - 1) * clamp(2 * dot(Dir, outward normal), 0, 1)`: full bonus inside 60 degrees of straight in, none along the boards or away.
   Key `game.contact.board_bonus` default 1.25, range 1..2, **neutral 1.0** (factor exactly 1.0: impulse bit-identical, no read-out, no event).
   The attacker's recoil test reads the impulse WITHOUT the bonus (the boards answer the victim, not the attacker).
2. **Board read-out**: on a landed hit (stumble or down) with factor > 1: victim `bBoardHitThisFrame` (B's `PlayBoardHit`), `bContactBoard`, counter `ContactBoardHits`, and a
   `board` event (Who = victim, Kind `skater`, Strength = the check's strength = closing / skater.speed, Where = nearest point of the boards, Dir = normal into the rink):
   EffectsBinder's glass wobble and board sound play. The victim's later slide into the boards within 30 frames is the same hit: its own `board` event is suppressed
   (`LastContactBoardFrame`, a read-out, never hashed; gates the event and the take only).
3. **Read-out `ContactBy`** (attacker index on the victim, per frame): a stumble emits no event naming its victim; hit-stop, camera and HUD find both ends with it.
4. **Hit-stop by outcome and ratio** (`AJamSkater::HitStopFramesFor`, called by `HokejGameMode::HitStopRoute`): at `jam.hitstop` 3 (default): stumble 1 frame, 2 from the middle
   of the stumble band (ratio >= 0.7 at defaults); knockdown lerp 2..5 over ratio 1..3 (`down_ratio`..`down_full_ratio`); the human's own bounce 1; bump 0. `jam.hitstop` scales
   (x base / 3, clamp 1..6), 0 = off. A knockdown the momentum model did not resolve (neutral contact) holds the base (3) as before.
5. **Camera kick for a stumble** (`UJamFollowCamera::StumbleKickScale`, `ContactKickObserve`): 3 cm at `bump_ratio` to 6 cm at `down_ratio` (a knockdown's smallest kick is 6 cm,
   biggest 12); stumble taken by the human, stumble it lands (the `check` event used to kick it like a knockdown), and its own bounce (6 cm, against its travel).
6. **HUD contact line** (`UJamHudWidget::UpdateContactLine`): bottom centre above the stamina bar, 60 frames, last 15 fade; "NOT WITH THE PUCK" when the human's `ChecksRefused`
   counter grows, "BOUNCED OFF" when its `ContactRecoils` grows (counters, so a frame the hit-stop held is not lost).
