## 2026-11-12 23:13 CEST interim (30 min): complaint 1 diagnosed, all five fixes written, first Game build running

**Complaint 1 root cause (the mouse never reaches the stick in the package).** `AJamMenuGameMode::PostLogin` (`MenuGameMode.cpp:47-52`) puts the player in
`FInputModeGameAndUI` with the cursor shown. That mode sets the game viewport's mouse capture to *capture during mouse down only*, and the `UGameViewportClient`
survives the `OpenLevel` into the Rink map, where nothing sets a match input mode (the only `FInputModeGameOnly` calls are the pause menu's `Resume()` and
`QuitToMenu()`). `FSceneViewport::OnMouseMove` accumulates `MouseDelta` (the source of the `MouseX` / `MouseY` axis events, hence of `Mouse2D` -> `IA_StickMouse`)
**only while the viewport has mouse capture**, so in the package the stick moved only while a mouse button was held down; the accumulator then kept whatever it had
(a stuck aim, often behind the skater). PIE starts on the Rink map, never passes the menu, and gets the ini default `CapturePermanently_IncludingInitialMouseDown`:
that is why the mouse worked in every PIE proof and never in the shipped build. After one Esc + Resume the package worked by accident (GameOnly = permanent capture).
Not a Shipping guard, not the cook, not A39's / A42's bindings (checked: `IMC_Hokej` is built in code, all keys present).

**Fix (written, building):** `AHokejGameMode::EnsureMatchInputMode` on possess and after every Resume (game+UI, cursor shown, locked to the viewport, the HUD
hit-test invisible); the stick aim now comes from the **cursor deprojected onto the ice** (the blade points at the cursor, rink-exact; dead zone 0.5 / 0.7 m, |Stick|
0.5..1 over 0.5..3 m), the right stick first and the accumulated delta as the fallback when the cursor is hidden; `JAMCTRL frame= stick=(x,y) src=mouse|pad|mousedelta`
per frame under `jam.ctrl.Log 1`, button edges always.

Also written: the operator's bindings (LMB shoot, RMB check, MMB / mouse 4 cross-check, wheel or Q deke, Shift sprint, Space poke, E pass, Ctrl brake; gamepad RT / RB /
LB / LT / A / X / Y / B brake); `game.skater.sprint { bonus 0.15, drain_frames 180, refill_frames 360 }` in the sim (integer stamina ticks, hashed only once used);
complaint 3's cause and fix in `AJamSkater::SmoothAimDeg` (the +-180 wrap of the camera-space aim behind the skater flipped the +-70 clamp every frame: side hysteresis,
15 deg/frame slew, 6-frame approach); complaint 2's fix in `StepThirdPerson` (yaw within 12 deg of the bearing to the pivot, pitch within 6.5 deg of the chest, `far_net_bias`
0.2 and `end_height_m` 3 by default) with a `JAMCAM` per-frame line under `jam.camera.Log 1`; complaint 5's calibration (human check window 12 frames, the human's
check lands from 2 m/s; bots unchanged, so the scripted baseline should not move) and a `jam.ctrl.Drill` scripted FJamInput drill for the packaged proof.
