## The design (20:47), all ON, each with a neutral value
- `game.pass.power.full_frames` 30 (0 = off, the neutral): the human's RMB hold is the power. A hold of shot.tap_frames (4) or fewer is the tap at skater.pass_speed 14 (arrives ~9 m/s at 9 m);
  from there the speed grows evenly with the hold to the full speed at 30 frames (0.5 s). Full speed is SOLVED from the rink: the speed that carries
  `pass.power.reach_share` 0.333 of the rink's length (30 m on the 90 m rink) and still moves `pass.power.arrive_mps` 7 there (drag + friction closed form) = ~23 m/s on the regular rink.
  The saucer under power: not a hold any more (the hold is power): it is given by the sim when a blade is in the flat lane and the saucer lane is open (a hand finds nothing to learn; rebinding unchanged).
  Bots' (and scripts') edge pass: the speed solved so it arrives at arrive_mps at the man (clamped to tap..full).
- `game.pass.aim.cone_deg` 25 (0 = off): the human's pass goes to the mate nearest the stick's line (blade direction; facing when the stick is at rest) inside 25 degrees, led by his velocity and the flight time; nobody in the cone = along the stick, it still goes.
- The lead under power: aimed at his BLADE where it will be when the puck gets there (flight solved with drag and friction), no pass_lead_frames on top.
- `game.pass.catch_m` 1.2 (0 = off): a pass in flight (new puck state PassFrames/PassBy, hashed) is seated on a team-mate's blade passing within 1.2 m of it at any speed over 1 m/s (today: 0.6 m and over 6 m/s). Opponents keep today's rules (a pass into an opponent's aimed stick is his).
- `game.pass.clear_own_body` true (false = neutral): the passer's (and shooter's) body does not stop his own puck while his cooldown runs.
- `game.shot.speed_scale` 2 (1 = neutral): the shot's speed (tap, charge bonus) and the puck's speed cap x2: 36 / 44 / 52 m/s.
