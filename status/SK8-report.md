## 5. The contact plane

- **New `M_SkaterContact`**: unlit, translucent, blend mode Modulate, before depth of field, no depth write. Colour = 1 falling to `Darkest` (parameter, 0.45) at the centre, smooth round falloff; the plane's scale makes it an ellipse.
- **`ContactShadow`** in `BP_SkaterVisual_TB1_P3` and `BP_SkaterVisual_TB2_P3`: a static mesh component (`/Engine/BasicShapes/Plane`), child of the `Mesh` component with no socket. The Blueprints turn the `Mesh` component for the facing, so the plane follows position and yaw, not bones. Relative place (3, 0, 0.4) cm, scale 0.5 along the facing and 0.7 across (50 x 70 cm). No shadow, `NoCollision`, no overlaps, no decals. Added through the Blueprint's component tree only; no graph touched.
- **In the game:** a real clay match (`generated_b2`) spawns six `BP_SkaterVisual_TB2_P3_C`, each with its plane at ice height under the figure. On `TB1_P3` seen on the six mirrored figures.
- **Effect** (`sk8-clay-contact-ab`): the ice under the feet darkens to 0.61 to 0.65 of its screen value at the darkest point. It is a soft patch between and around the skates, clearly there in the close view, faint from the game camera.
- **What it does not do:** the amber stain is the figure's cast shadow, and the lamp angle puts it 0.5 to 1 m to the side of the figure. A 50 x 70 cm plane under the pelvis does not cover it. The stain is still there.
- **Knocked down:** the plane stays under the figure's position and reads as a soft dark patch by the lying body (`sk8-clay-knockdown-contact`). Left visible; hiding it would need a graph change.
- **Other tiers:** `HokejGameMode.cpp` lines 167 to 173 pick `BP_SkaterVisual_TB2_P3` only for `generated_b2` on the clay tier, and never pick `BP_SkaterVisual_TB1_P3`. The night figure's Blueprint is byte-identical. So no other tier can show the plane.
- **One trap for whoever edits the material:** setting Modulate while the material is still lit fails to compile ("Dynamically lit translucency is not supported") and the plane then draws nothing until the material is recompiled. It is saved unlit and compiled; after a cold start it loads with no error and draws.
