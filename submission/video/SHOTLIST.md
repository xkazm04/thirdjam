Device B's recipe, written and not yet run: the system's own game recorder at 60 fps with game sound, the booth's voice off for pass 1 and on for pass 2 (`status/TRAILER-report.md`, "Filming recipe"). ffmpeg on device B cannot record the game's sound; this machine has no capture device at all.

- **The cut is one command** on device B: set each shot's `src`, `in` and `out` in `A45.json` (or `A30.json`, `C45.json`), run `edit.mjs` on it; the last line must read length_ok true, 1920x1080@60, overruns 0, check PASS.

Whether a split frame of two Development takes may sit inside a film whose other shots are Shipping is device B's open question 5 (`[O17]`).
- **Fallback film**: B's scenario C (any one recorded match of season take 1 or of PLAY NOW at PRO becomes a film; `C45.json`).
