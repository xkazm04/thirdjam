# HOKEJ

**Hockey where you direct the rules.**

HOKEJ is a wild five-a-side arcade hockey game with no referee and no mercy. Play it clean and win with passes, timing and a smart
stop-and-go. Or play it dirty: flatten the other team with your shoulder, your stick, or a shotgun blast that clears the whole lane.
Both ways win. And when the match is not going your way, you are the Director: pause the game and change a rule.

## Why you will want one more match

- **Two ways to win.** Out-skate and out-pass them, or knock them down and take the puck off the ice they are lying on. The game never
  tells you which one is right.
- **Hits that mean something.** The faster you arrive, the harder they fall: a nudge side by side, a full knockdown head-on. Come in
  slow and you bounce off. Carry the puck and you are the one being hunted.
- **Your mouse is your stick.** The blade follows your cursor. Tap to shoot flat, hold to lift it over a sliding defender. Point your
  stick at a teammate and pass; hold the button for a harder one.
- **You direct the match.** Once a period, hit pause and move one dial: how fast everyone skates, how hard the shot flies, how slippery
  the ice is, how sharp the opponents are. Press APPLY and the scoreboard announces the new rule to both teams.
- **Twelve modes that break hockey on purpose.** Three against five. One goal ends it. A blast that floors everyone in its path. Ice so
  big you run out of legs.
- **Make your own.** Type one sentence ("tiny rink, huge nets, everybody sprints") and the Designer builds a game from it.
- **Teammates and opponents that play as a team.** Your side passes to you and chases loose pucks. The other side carries, passes when
  you close in, and comes for you when you have the puck.

## Twelve modes

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Arsenal** | Grab a boost on the ice and get a trick: a shotgun, a hip check, a shield or a slow puck. Each one fires three times. |
| **Bruisers** | Heavy bodies, five a side. Hit everything. |
| **Cage** | A tight box, eight skaters, four pillars in the way. The boards hit back. |
| **Fog of War** | Three a side at night, in fog, against opponents who see you late. Slip by, then strike. |
| **Glow Ice** | A dark rink drawn in neon light, and the only mode with penalties: foul and you sit ten seconds. |
| **Marathon** | Three on three on endless ice. Bring your legs. |
| **Outnumbered** | Three against five. Keep the puck, take the heat. Your trick is a shield. |
| **Pond** | Outdoor pond hockey under open sky. Pass, skate, nobody gets hit. |
| **Power Play** | Five against three, and the five are yours. Move it, fire it, repeat. |
| **Shotgun** | Tight ice, ten bodies, and one blast that clears the lane. Anyone near its path goes down. Your own side too. |
| **Sniper** | A big net and a low guard. Lift it, bury it. |
| **Sudden Death** | One goal ends the match. The meanest opponents in the game. |

Four of them play in a neon arena, each in its own colour; the rest move between a packed timber hall, night ice and a sunlit pond.

## Also in the box

- **PLAY NOW** at three levels: ROOKIE to learn, PRO for anyone who has played a hockey game before, ALL-STAR if you want a wall.
- **A season** of eight matches that starts as a school and ends as a fight.
- **PLAY WITH A FRIEND**: one of you hosts, the other types an invite code, both on the same network. Experimental: we have run it
  as two copies on one machine, never yet across two.
- **A commentary booth** with a line for most things that happen to you, shown as captions.
- **A controls panel** on screen all match that shows what your buttons do right now, with or without the puck. It follows your
  rebinds, and you can switch it off in SETTINGS.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Pad |
|---|---|---|
| Skate | W A S D or arrows | left stick |
| Aim the stick | mouse | right stick |
| Shoot (tap flat, hold to lift) / Cross-check without the puck (hold to brace) | left mouse button | RT |
| Pass (hold for power) / Body check without the puck | right mouse button | RB |
| Trick or ability, when its slot is lit | F | R3 |
| Sprint | Shift | LT |
| Brake hard, then go | S against your motion, or Ctrl | stick back, or L3 |
| Poke the puck loose | Space | A |
| Spin off a defender / Deke | E / mouse wheel | B / Y |
| Pause, change a rule, rebind keys | Esc | Start |

Every key can be rebound under CONTROLS.

## How it was made

One person directed the whole game; AI coding agents wrote the code, the tests, the art pipeline and the build tools under that
direction, on two machines, in four days. Menu, HUD and mode artwork were made with AI image generation, the skater figure
with AI 3D tools, the commentary and sound with AI audio tools; all of it is declared as generated in the credits.
Built with Unreal Engine 5.9. The full dev log, turn by turn, is here: [DEV LOG LINK]

## How to run

Unzip `Hokej_Hokej.zip` (about 283 MB) anywhere and run `ThirdJam.exe`.

- **Windows 10 or 11, DirectX 12** graphics card. No web build. On RTX graphics cards it uses NVIDIA DLSS technology.
- The first launch takes a few seconds to prepare.
- Windows may ask about the firewall once. Solo play needs no network; PLAY WITH A FRIEND does.
- MAKE YOUR OWN writes new games only with your own AI key, typed in SETTINGS; no key ships with the game. Without one it offers
  games it made earlier.

## Rough edges, said plainly

- Close to the side boards the camera gets steep and tight.
- The gamepad is mapped but has not been play-tested by a person; mouse and keyboard is the way to play.
- ROOKIE is easy on purpose and ALL-STAR is hard on purpose; PRO is where a match is even.
- At PRO you will spend time flat on the ice. That is the other team playing the same game you are.
