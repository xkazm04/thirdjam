THE BAR (docs/HOCKEY.md section 6, prep-reference/STYLE-PATHS.md P2 T2): "painted cel toy hockey at night". A night arena with the house lights down: four shadowed spot cones make soft light pools on scuffed ice with painted-looking brush streaks and frost patches; a deep-blue night dome (not a black void) above a dark ceiling line; emissive trim in the two team colours (deep blue, warm orange) along the boards; boards with two drawn lines (an ochre kick plate at the foot, a dark glass rail on top); red nets drawn as thick outlined frames; no crowd, no goalies, no referees. Style: painted cel, meaning hand-painted-looking flat textures, visible ink-like outlines whose width falls with distance, flat cel lighting steps (two or three bands), a limited palette of five or six colours plus one accent, painted backdrop cards. Figures: chunky toy proportions (short, wide, round head, big hands, a stick with a visible blade), not athletes; two jersey colours that separate on the ice at every distance. The puck is small and near-black and survives distance only through a saturated glowing disc under it and a trail. The far net must be findable in under a second (red frame, a screen marker in the game HUD).
