What this is. 1340 trash-talk lines the game shows over a skater when something happens (a check, a goal, a stall). Opponents mock, complain, brag or, on the Raw cut, go savage; teammates encourage, complain or praise. Situations are seen from you, the human player. Listen to a line or read it, then pass or reject it; add a comment where a rewrite would help.

The bar you asked for (R-0015). "More offensive with the mockery, the current scripts are too safe and childish." So the register is cutting sarcasm about your skating, aim, speed, courage and decisions, the bored voice of a veteran, genuine annoyance from teammates. It is not a kids' show.

Hockey first (R-0021). "We are pushing the theme too far, it should be hockey first theme, keeping director cut in the menu." 118 lines that leaned on film words (cut, take, director, script, scene, reel, blooper...) are now rink talk at the same register, same id and same mild budget; they are tagged rewritten and show the old text in their note, so search for rewritten to judge them. Your earlier decisions on the other lines still apply.

What the filter already forbids. Slurs of any kind, sexual content, threats of real harm, real people, brands, teams, bodies or identities, anything aimed at who you are rather than how you play, drugs and gambling, politics, religion, URLs, emoji. Lines are 6 to 60 characters. Things this game does not have (goalie, referee, crowd, coach, penalty) are out.

Mild language. Allowed sparingly: damn, hell, crap, and "ass" only in "kick your ass", never two in a line. 56 of 1340 lines carry a mild word (4.2%, quota 10%); they are tagged mild so you can find them. Mild lines are the usual rejects if the Teen bar worries you.

What happens to your decisions. Rejected lines are removed from the bank, comments are logged in the review file, the content filter runs again and the bank is rebuilt into the game. A group that drops under 12 lines gets topped up by the generator.
