# Theme: DIRECTOR'S CUT

Announced at the kickoff stream on 2026-11-11 (relayed by the operator from the organisers' post on X; the itch page still showed the pre-kickoff text at 10:00 CET on 11-12):

> This year's MegaJam Theme is... DIRECTOR'S CUT! Your vision, your specific edits and ultimately: your final cut - your Director's Cut.
> Every developer has that one feature that didn't quite make it, the mechanic scoped out while sleep deprived at 2 AM or that idea you've had shelved and never
> quite found a place for it yet. Well, now's your chance to finally build it all in this year's Epic MegaJam.
> If you're utilizing AI agents in your workflows, you're directing those too! Set the vision, delegate your tasks and decide what goes in and what doesn't.
> Aim for ambition and direct your path accordingly!

## How Hokej uses it (rule 7(g): the theme must be visible to a judge in the first 30 seconds)

1. **The game is a set of cuts.** Every playable variation is a cartridge called a *cut*: `Regular Cut`, `Raw Cut` (no rules, body checks), `Boost Cut` (pickups),
   `Small Rink Cut`, and the player's own. The main menu is a film-slate: "Choose your cut".
2. **The player is the director.** The Designer layer is the theme made playable: the player types a sentence between periods ("make it faster and meaner",
   "a calm version for my kid", "twice the rink, half the friction") and the Designer edits the cut live (D0 presets always; D1 live with a key). The result is
   saved as `My Director's Cut` and can be replayed. That is "your vision, your specific edits, your final cut".
3. **The twist (one rule only this theme makes sense of):** *Director's Call*: once per period the player can pause the match and change one tunable on the spot
   (rink friction, shot power, number of opponents, pickup rate); the change is announced on the scoreboard as a *cut* ("Director's Call: ice is now glass").
   Data over existing systems (the cartridge and the Designer), no new system.
4. **Skin:** film-slate UI, "TAKE 1 / TAKE 2" period names, a clapperboard faceoff sound, scoreboard as a film counter, replay of the last goal as "the cut".
5. **The dev log** (Glass Box) is literally a director's log: the operator directing two machines of agents, the briefs, the cuts that went in and the ones that did not.

Judging note: AI use is "context only"; the Designer earns points as a gameplay feature (Ambition, Gameplay, Theme), not as a badge.
